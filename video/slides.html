RFID-Based Smart Attendance
& Door Access Control

One card tap opens the door and writes the attendance record — and the system notices when one person taps several cards.

ESP32 · MFRC522 reader · SG90 servo · weighted risk scoring

01 · Problem

Manual attendance is slow, and cards alone cannot prove identity

  • Roll-call costs 5–10 minutes of every class and invites transcription errors.
  • A card proves a card was scanned, not who was holding it.
  • Proxy attendance: one student taps five friends' cards — five students marked present.
  • A normal card log records the cheat perfectly and never sees it.
The unsolved part is not the door. It is the pattern of taps.
02 · Objective

Automate the record, control the door, and flag the pattern

The system does

  • reads the card UID locally, in under a second
  • opens the latch only for a registered card
  • writes one time-stamped attendance entry per session
  • works with no internet, uploading records later

The system refuses to do

  • never marks anyone absent on its own
  • never locks out a registered student
  • never accuses — it flags a run for a human
  • never invents a timestamp it did not have

07 · The detection idea

A real queue and a stack of cards look different in time

Real queue

  • one person every 2–3 s
  • card, step aside, next person
  • gaps are human walking speed
Nothing to flag.

Stack of cards

  • five taps in under 1.2 s
  • gaps of 0.2–0.3 s
  • a hand can move that fast; two people cannot pass one door that fast
Flag the run, do not accuse the student.

The threshold: two different cards closer than 0.5 s at one door.

11 · Honest limits and future scope

What this cannot do yet

Limits, stated plainly

  • a cloned card carries the same UID — invisible at UID level
  • one door cannot prove travel time; a patient cheat lands in WATCH
  • the 8-session evaluation set is synthetic and labelled
  • physical evidence is two cards over two days

Next

  • a second reader at a second door → impossible travel becomes provable
  • DESFire / challenge-response → cloning stops working
  • a term of real sessions to re-run the evaluation
  • signed records so the log cannot be edited afterwards
